# Overview

The day-by-day history of raised, taken, skipped and acknowledged events and the doses still scheduled,
and the reminders the app raises to fill it.

## Boundary

Owns raising reminders and answering them, correcting, deleting and re-raising events and logging
additional doses. It does not own how a reminder is scheduled, which a medicine's reminders define, nor
the analysis of the history, export or backup.

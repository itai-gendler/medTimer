# Edit reminder

The User changes a reminder: its time and dosage on the medicine, and in its Advanced settings whether it is enabled, its active date range, dosing instructions, cycle, weekdays and days of month, interval, dosage entry, automatic taking, notification priority, or its stock threshold or days before expiration.
